## 1. Introduction

MultiUserFullBody ("the Application") is a multiplayer virtual
reality application for Meta Quest devices developed by Mason
Prather ("the Developer").

The Application allows users to interact in shared virtual
environments using virtual avatars, voice communication,
tracked movements, and shared photos and videos.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application
processes, how that information is used, when it may be shared,
and how users can request access to or deletion of applicable
personal information.

## 2. Information We Process

The Application processes information necessary to provide
its virtual reality and multiplayer functionality.

### 2.1 Multiplayer and Account Information

The Application uses Unity Gaming Services Authentication
and Unity Multiplayer Services.

Information processed may include:

- Automatically generated Unity player identifiers.
- Authentication and session credentials.
- Player display names.
- Multiplayer room names and identifiers.
- Room membership and join codes.
- Network connection information.
- Session configuration and connection status.
- IP addresses processed by networking services.

The Application uses anonymous Unity Authentication.
The inspected authentication implementation does not request
a personal name, email address, or password.

Display names may be visible to other multiplayer participants.

### 2.2 Virtual Reality Tracking Information

The Application processes tracking information from supported
Meta Quest devices.

Depending on device capabilities and permissions, this may
include:

- Head position and orientation.
- Hand and controller movements.
- Finger and hand-tracking information.
- Body position and movement.
- Supported facial expression information.
- Supported eye-pose information.
- Controller inputs and interaction states.

This information is used to animate avatars, support virtual
interactions, and synchronize movements during multiplayer
sessions.

Derived avatar animation information is transmitted to other
participants. Supported facial, eye, and lip animations may
be included in the synchronized avatar representation.

The inspected Application configuration uses bundled Meta
Avatar assets rather than requesting the user's personal
Meta account avatar.

### 2.3 Voice Communication

The Application uses Unity Vivox for multiplayer voice chat.

Information processed for voice functionality may include:

- Microphone audio.
- Voice participant identifiers.
- Voice channel identifiers.
- Voice activity and connection status.
- Virtual head position and orientation for spatial audio.

Microphone permission is requested when required for voice
functionality.

Users can mute their microphone using the Application's
supported controls.

Voice audio may also be processed locally to generate avatar
lip-sync animations.

The inspected implementation does not intentionally record
or transcribe voice communications at the application level.

Unity Vivox may process voice and connection information
according to its own service practices.

### 2.4 Photos, Videos, and Shared Media

The Application allows users to import, upload, display, and
share selected photos and videos.

Information processed may include:

- User-selected image and video content.
- Filenames and file formats.
- Media dimensions and orientation.
- File sizes and transfer metadata.
- Media previews and playback information.

Users may upload media from compatible devices through a
local network connection.

Media selected for sharing may be transmitted to other
participants through Unity multiplayer networking services.

Other participants may receive and retain copies of shared
media.

### 2.5 Network and Device Information

The Application uses local networking and Unity networking
services to support multiplayer interactions and media uploads.

Information processed may include:

- Local and public IP addresses, as applicable.
- Network connection identifiers.
- Network endpoint information.
- HTTP request metadata.
- Media-upload pairing codes.
- Connection and transfer diagnostics.

The local media-upload feature uses HTTP on the user's
local network.

Users are encouraged to use this feature only on trusted
networks.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Application is used to:

- Authenticate multiplayer participants.
- Create, discover, and join multiplayer sessions.
- Synchronize avatars and virtual interactions.
- Enable real-time voice communication.
- Generate spatial audio and avatar lip synchronization.
- Process VR tracking and movement.
- Import, display, and share selected media.
- Support local-network media uploads.
- Maintain application functionality and troubleshoot errors.

The Developer does not intentionally use personal information
for targeted advertising, independent marketing, or the sale
of personal data.

## 4. Information Shared With Other Users

During multiplayer sessions, other participants may receive
or observe:

- Player display names.
- Avatar appearance and animation.
- Head, hand, and body movements represented by avatars.
- Supported facial and lip-sync animation.
- Voice communications.
- Virtual interaction states.
- Photos and videos shared with the room.

Information required for multiplayer synchronization may
be transmitted through Unity networking infrastructure.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive or confidential media.

The Developer cannot guarantee deletion of content already
received or retained by other multiplayer participants.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

The Application may store certain information locally on
the user's Meta Quest device.

Locally stored information may include:

- Uploaded photos and videos.
- Imported media.
- Videos received through multiplayer sharing.
- Application configuration information.
- Authentication information managed through Unity services.
- Diagnostic information generated during operation.

Uploaded media and certain received files may remain in
the Application's persistent storage after a session ends.

Other information, including multiplayer animation data,
may be temporarily held in memory.

The Developer does not intentionally maintain an independent
cloud database of users' personal avatar profiles or
multiplayer activity histories.

Meta and Unity may process or retain information according
to their respective service practices and privacy policies.

The exact retention periods for provider-managed information
are determined by the relevant services.

## 7. Passthrough and Environmental Information

The Application may use Meta Quest passthrough functionality
to display the user's physical surroundings.

Passthrough imagery is displayed using supported Meta
platform mechanisms.

The inspected implementation does not independently extract
or upload raw passthrough camera frames.

Device tracking information may still be processed for
supported virtual reality interactions.

## 8. Data Security

The Developer seeks to limit unnecessary collection,
transmission, and exposure of user information.

The Application relies on Meta and Unity platform
technologies for supported networking and virtual reality
features.

Users should understand that:

- Multiplayer features transmit information over networks.
- Voice communication is processed through Unity Vivox.
- Shared media may be received and retained by other users.
- Local media uploads use HTTP rather than HTTPS.
- No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should use trusted local networks and avoid sharing
sensitive information through multiplayer features.
